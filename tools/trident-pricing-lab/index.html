Trident Pricing lab

LOCAL PLANNING TOOL · ASSUMPTIONS, NOT SALES

What could the offer earn?

Compare three price points. Change conversion and support costs to see what has to be true.

All inputs are illustrative assumptions. Recurring revenue is normalised per month; it is not cash collected. Costs exclude your time unless entered. Fees, taxes, refunds, churn and acquisition spend are not modelled.

Test the offer, then test the product.

  1. Pick one audience: independent builders or freelancers who repeatedly reconstruct a project after an interruption.
  2. Show a consistent named offer and the same product explanation. Record the exact price, audience, source and date of each voluntary response.
  3. Ask what project they would try, what they expect to get and which offer they would choose. Interest is weaker evidence than a completed purchase.
  4. Once public distribution, payment handling and terms are ready, compare purchases, successful setup, a return session and support effort.
  5. Keep the offer if paid users return and support costs are workable. Change the promise or workflow if they cannot reach the result.

No outreach, tracking, checkout or experiment assignment runs from this tool. Nothing is saved or sent. A revenue calculation does not establish demand.
