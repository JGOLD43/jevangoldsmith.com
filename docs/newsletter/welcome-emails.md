# Welcome emails

Status: ready to configure after an email provider is connected. These emails have not been scheduled or sent.

Use the provider's subscriber-confirmed trigger if double opt-in is enabled. Each person should enter this sequence once. Stop delivery immediately on unsubscribe. Add the account's verified sender details and the provider's unsubscribe footer; do not invent a postal address.

Subject: Welcome — here's a look inside

Hi,

Thanks for joining me.

Once a month, I'll send you a short update on my projects, something I've learned, and a useful book, tool or idea.
