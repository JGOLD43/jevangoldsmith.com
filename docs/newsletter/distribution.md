## Measurement

Establish the baseline once analytics and the email service are connected. Missing numbers mean unknown, not zero.

| Metric | Definition | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New confirmed subscribers | New unique subscribed addresses after any required confirmation | Email provider |
| Visitor-to-subscriber conversion | Confirmed subscribers attributed to the site divided by unique site visitors, for the same period | Email provider + site analytics |
| Discovery source | Subscriber counts by source, medium, campaign and landing page | Signup metadata / provider fields |
| Interest in projects | Unique email clicks to project and Now pages | Email provider click report |
| Retention | Unsubscribes and engaged subscribers over time | Email provider |

Do not count a form button click or an inbox submission as a confirmed subscriber. Review results weekly; compare like periods before deciding which story or signup placement to change.
