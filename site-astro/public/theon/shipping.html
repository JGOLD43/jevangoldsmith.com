HOW TO READ THIS

Evidence with its limits.

A fixed denominator.

Completion is accepted outcomes divided by this cycle’s agreed outcomes. It is not a percentage of the whole super app.

Elapsed cycle time.

The median of recorded start-to-acceptance intervals. Waiting is included. Missing dates remain unavailable.

Known observations.

No historical Mac delivery rate is invented from version numbers or inherited Android records. Zero recorded regressions does not establish zero bugs.
