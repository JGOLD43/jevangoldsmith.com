A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you begin.

Something else on your mind?
Join the conversation

What is Trident?

A private, open-source Mac workspace for picking up projects, chosen learning and personal plans where you left off. Start with a next step, a draft and a useful reference; use the broader collections when they help you.

Can I download it today?

You can build the current Mac app from source using the build guide. A public notarized Mac installer is being prepared. The supported paid editions shown above are proposals; they are not available to purchase yet.

Why pay if it’s open source?

The proposed paid offer is convenient distribution, guided setup, ongoing maintenance and support. The core source remains open. Pricing is being explored before a supported edition launches.

Does the founding licence mean lifetime updates?

No. The proposed licence lets you keep the purchased major version and includes the first year of updates and support. Future major versions and optional services would have their own terms. No purchase or renewal is available on this page.

Do I need an AI subscription?

No. Local projects, writing, reading and personal records do not require AI. Optional assistance needs a separately configured provider and may have its own subscription or usage cost; no model allowance is included in the proposed prices.

What stays private?

The personal workspace is encrypted locally, and external websites run separately from it. AI and connected services have their own boundaries. Choose what context to share and review the security guide. This marketing page has no forms collecting personal information or advertising analytics.

Is this also a phone or web app?

This offer is for the independent Mac application. An older Android companion and private website library exist separately. Their setup and compatibility are distinct; these proposed Mac plans do not promise a new mobile release or seamless cloud sync.
