01 / A MORE CONNECTED DAY

A lot lives here.
It should work well together.

Theon already has breadth. We’re building depth: dependable paths from saved context to something useful, learned, or enjoyed.

01

Work that resumes.

Bring a task, draft, browser page and saved reference into one compact workspace. Leave with a result you can keep.

Today · Planning · Writing · Browser
02

Learning that sticks.

Choose a skill, read with context, attempt it independently and return for a delayed check. Collecting lessons is only the beginning.

Reader · Skill trees · Practice
03

Attention you can see.

Notice elapsed time and observed app use. Compare your week with chosen results, with gaps in coverage made visible.

Today · Week · Time & attention
04

A life beyond tasks.

Keep useful context for relationships, meals, training, trips and your personal library. Ordinary enjoyment belongs here too.

People · Health · Trips · Library
05

Saved once. Found again.

Keep photos, videos, links, notes and documents in Spaces. Correct local sorting and reopen the material that matters to you.

Spaces · Capture · Search
06

AI with a clear boundary.

Use optional assistance with selected context and inspect saved changes. Provider choices and media support are still growing.

Selected sharing · Actions · Undo
